# MediaFlow 5.1.0.564

The app now matches the MediaFlow website **v564**.

## Task progress
- The **progress bar shows again**. Its fill had no height, so it never appeared.
- Animated like the website:
  - a deeper track with an inner shadow
  - a fill that eases to its width and glows
  - a gradient that flows across it every 3 seconds
  - light streaks every 2.8 seconds
  - a glossy top and a capped goal marker
- The background matches the website too: its two wave layers drift in opposite directions (9 s and 13 s), the aura breathes over 6 seconds, and the left side fades so the numbers stay readable.
- The numbers and labels use the website's sizes, and the percentage glows. Every animation runs on its own clock, so none of them jumps when it loops.

## Themes
- Themes now show **their own colours by default**: your selected theme, or the Dynamic Cover Theme, is no longer replaced by plain light or dark surfaces.
- Turn on **Global Light / Dark appearance** in Settings → Themes to apply Light or Dark to any theme.
- Your account is moved over once, on both the app and the website. After that the switch is yours.
- The app and website now pick the same Light or Dark, including for the Dynamic Cover Theme.
