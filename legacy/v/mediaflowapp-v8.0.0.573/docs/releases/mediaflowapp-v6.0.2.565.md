# MediaFlow 6.0.2.565

Two small Discord updates.

## Consuming…
- On the Log page it now says **Consuming** and the title: the title you've pinned or, when nothing is pinned, the title you're logging.
  - **Pin** keeps showing the title you're logging on Discord.
  - The swap button picks another title.
- Under it, the **Discord preview** shows exactly what Discord shows right now, the same card as in Settings → Discord.

## Discord
- New **Show progress** setting (Settings → Discord). Turn it off to show only the title and what you're doing, without the episode, chapter, total or percent.
