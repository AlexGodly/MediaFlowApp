# MediaFlow 6.0.2.565

A small update to **Consuming…** on the Log page.

- It now says **Consuming** and the title: the title you've pinned or, when nothing is pinned, the title you're logging.
  - **Pin** keeps showing the title you're logging on Discord.
  - The swap button picks another title.
- Under it, the **Discord preview** shows exactly what Discord shows right now, the same card as in Settings → Discord.
