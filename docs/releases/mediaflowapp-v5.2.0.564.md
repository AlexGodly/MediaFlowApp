# MediaFlow 5.2.0.564

The app still matches the MediaFlow website **v564**.

## Discord
- **Link your Discord account on Windows too.** Settings → Discord now has **Link Discord account** on desktop. Once linked, your status shows even when the Discord app is closed. Without a link it still works through the Discord app, as before.
- **Your status stays when MediaFlow is in the background** on phones:
  - Android keeps MediaFlow running with a quiet "Showing your status on Discord" notification while Discord is on and linked. Turn Discord off in Settings to remove it.
  - iOS keeps the status until the system suspends MediaFlow. iOS doesn't let apps stay running in the background for this.
- **Idle shows on every device**, including while MediaFlow is in the background.
- New idle choice: **Never show idle (keep my status)**. Your current status stays however long you're away.

## XP
- App time (and its XP) now keeps counting while MediaFlow is in the background or you're idle. Only closing MediaFlow stops it. Time when the device is asleep still doesn't count.
